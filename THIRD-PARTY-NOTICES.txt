THIRD-PARTY MEDIA AND SOURCE NOTICES
====================================

The application interface, diagrams, icon and splash artwork in this package
are original project assets.

The app can load selected archaeological photographs from Wikimedia Commons.
Those photographs are not embedded in this ZIP. Each artifact card provides a
link to its source record and a rights note. Current source records include:

- Stele of Hammurabi, Louvre Sb 8:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Code_of_Hammurabi_Louvre_Museum.jpg

- Mesha Stele, Louvre AO 5066, photograph by Tangopaso:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mesha_stele_(Louvre,_AO_5066).jpg

- Ahiram sarcophagus inscription, photograph by Onceinawhile, CC BY-SA 4.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:National_Museum_of_Beirut_%E2%80%93_Ahiram_sarcophagus_inscription_2.jpg

- Ugaritic Baal Cycle tablet, photograph by Mbzt, CC BY-SA 3.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:P1180250_Louvre_Langue_ougaritique_Tablette_poeme_mythologique_AO16641_et_16642_rwk.jpg

- Palmyrene funerary inscription, Louvre AO 2205, photograph by Marie-Lan
  Nguyen, public-domain dedication:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Inscription_Palmyra_Louvre_AO2205.jpg

Licenses and attribution details can change or be clarified on the source
record. Verify the source page before redistributing images or producing a
commercial publication.
